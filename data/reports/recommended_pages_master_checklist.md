# Master List of Website Pages for Your Telehealth Compounded GLP-1 Business

> **Strategic Website Page Architecture & Deployment Roadmap**  
> **Business Model:** Telehealth Consultations & Home Delivery of Compounded Semaglutide and Compounded Tirzepatide  
> **Source Intelligence:** Synthesized from top-performing competitor websites (*Mochi Health, Ro, OrderlyMeds, IVÍM Health, TryEden, JoinFridays, Lavender Sky Health*).

---

## Page Priority Overview

To launch quickly without getting overwhelmed, your pages are organized into three rollout phases:

* **Phase 1: P0 — Launch Blockers (14 Core Pages):** Required to legally operate, take doctor consults, process card payments, and ship medication.
* **Phase 2: P1 — Trust, Credibility & E-E-A-T Accelerators (10 Pages):** Required to overcome customer skepticism, prove pharmacy accreditation, reduce churn, and prevent cart abandonment.
* **Phase 3: P2 — Organic SEO & Viral Growth Engine (9+ Pages):** Required to rank for high-intent Google searches, run creator/influencer funnels, and capture organic traffic without paid ads.

---

## Phase 1: P0 — Launch Blockers (Must Have on Day 1)

These 14 pages are non-negotiable for launching your telehealth service.

---

### 1. Homepage (`/`)
* **Objective:** Immediate value proposition, hook prospective patients within 5 seconds, address primary objections (cost, safety, needles), and push to the intake quiz.
* **Key Elements Needed:**
  - **Hero Section:** Clear headline (e.g., *"Doctor-Prescribed Compounded GLP-1s Delivered to Your Door"*).
  - **Price Hook:** Transparent flat-rate badge (e.g., *"Starting at $199/mo — No hidden fees, no titration price hikes"*).
  - **Trust Badges:** Licensed US 503A Compounding Pharmacies, LegitScript certified seal, HIPAA compliant, Board-Certified US Physicians.
  - **3-Step "How It Works" Bar:** 1. Take 90-sec Quiz → 2. Doctor Reviews & Prescribes → 3. Cold-Chain Delivery to Your Door.
  - **Medication Selection Cards:** Side-by-side comparison of Compounded Semaglutide vs Compounded Tirzepatide with starting prices.
  - **Primary CTA Buttons:** Multiple sticky *"Get Started / Take Quiz"* buttons linking to `/quiz`.

---

### 2. Asynchronous Qualification & Intake Quiz (`/quiz` or `/get-started`)
* **Objective:** Frictionless qualification funnel that collects patient data, calculates BMI, screens contraindications, and captures email/phone for cart recovery.
* **Key Elements Needed:**
  - **Step 1: State Selection:** Verifies your doctor has a medical license in the patient's state.
  - **Step 2: Biometrics:** Sex, height, current weight, goal weight (automatically computes BMI).
  - **Step 3: Medical Contraindications:** Screening for Medullary Thyroid Carcinoma (MTC), Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia type 2 (MEN 2), history of pancreatitis, gallbladder issues, or pregnancy/breastfeeding.
  - **Step 4: Medication Preference:** Asks whether they prefer Semaglutide (budget-friendly) or Tirzepatide (maximum weight loss), and Injection vs Oral.
  - **Step 5: Contact Capture:** Email & SMS capture *before* displaying doctor review options (for automated abandoned-cart recovery).

---

### 3. Compounded Semaglutide Treatment Landing Page (`/treatments/compounded-semaglutide`)
* **Objective:** The dedicated sales and clinical page for Semaglutide. Targets patients looking for generic Ozempic® / Wegovy®.
* **Key Elements Needed:**
  - **What It Is:** Explanation of GLP-1 receptor agonist mechanism (appetite reduction, delayed gastric emptying).
  - **Pricing & Inclusions:** All-inclusive monthly rate ($199–$249/mo) covering telehealth consult, medication, syringes, alcohol pads, and cold-chain shipping.
  - **Dosage Progression:** Visual titration timeline (0.25mg → 0.5mg → 1.0mg → 1.7mg → 2.4mg).
  - **Clinical Results:** Highlight the 15% average weight-loss benchmark from clinical trials.
  - **Compounding Clarification:** Clear disclosure that it is prepared by a licensed US compounding pharmacy.
  - **Direct CTA:** *"Claim My Semaglutide Plan"*.

---

### 4. Compounded Tirzepatide Treatment Landing Page (`/treatments/compounded-tirzepatide`)
* **Objective:** The high-ticket sales page for Tirzepatide. Targets patients seeking the dual GIP/GLP-1 generic Zepbound® / Mounjaro®.
* **Key Elements Needed:**
  - **Why Dual-Action Matters:** Explains how targeting *both* GIP and GLP-1 receptors yields superior weight reduction (up to 20–22% average weight loss).
  - **Pricing & Inclusions:** Flat-rate or tiered pricing ($349–$399/mo).
  - **Dosage Progression:** Visual titration chart (2.5mg → 5.0mg → 7.5mg → 10mg → 12.5mg → 15mg).
  - **Switching From Semaglutide:** Clear section explaining how patients on Semaglutide can safely switch to Tirzepatide if they hit a plateau.
  - **Direct CTA:** *"Start Tirzepatide Treatment"*.

---

### 5. Pricing & Plans Page (`/pricing`)
* **Objective:** Total financial transparency to kill the #1 customer fear: the "titration price trap."
* **Key Elements Needed:**
  - **Side-by-Side Comparison Table:**
    - Column 1: Compounded Semaglutide ($199–$249/mo flat rate).
    - Column 2: Compounded Tirzepatide ($349–$399/mo flat rate).
  - **Explicit "What's Included" Checklist:**
    - ✓ 100% Online consultation with US licensed provider.
    - ✓ Medication shipped in temperature-controlled packaging.
    - ✓ All injection supplies (syringes & alcohol pads).
    - ✓ Free anti-nausea medication (Zofran) if clinically indicated.
    - ✓ Ongoing dosage titration & provider messaging.
    - ✓ **NO** separate monthly membership fees.
    - ✓ **NO** hidden doctor consultation fees.
  - **Cancel Anytime Guarantee:** Explicit statement that there are no long-term contracts.

---

### 6. How It Works Page (`/how-it-works`)
* **Objective:** Demystify the telemedicine and home delivery process so first-time buyers feel safe.
* **Key Elements Needed:**
  - **Step 1: 90-Second Medical Intake:** Complete the online medical assessment from phone or laptop.
  - **Step 2: Board-Certified Physician Review:** A licensed clinician reviews health history within 24 hours.
  - **Step 3: Direct Home Delivery:** Partner pharmacy dispenses and ships refrigerated overnight.
  - **Step 4: Ongoing Care & Support:** Message your care team anytime, request dosage adjustments, and access refill schedules.

---

### 7. Pharmacy Partners & Sourcing Transparency (`/pharmacy-partners`)
* **Objective:** Build instant legitimacy by revealing your compounding pharmacy supply chain.
* **Key Elements Needed:**
  - **Accreditation Disclosure:** Explain that your partner pharmacies are FDA-registered 503A or 503B compounding facilities (e.g., Hallandale, Red Rock, Empower, Strive, Boothwyn).
  - **Quality Assurance:** Explain sterility testing, endotoxin testing, and third-party Certificate of Analysis (CoA) batch testing.
  - **Cold-Chain Guarantee:** Explain how medication is packed in insulated coolers with temperature-monitored gel ice packs.

---

### 8. Medical Team & Clinical Leadership (`/medical-team`)
* **Objective:** Establish Google E-E-A-T and show prospective patients that real US doctors oversee their treatment.
* **Key Elements Needed:**
  - **Medical Director Profile:** Headshot, full name, MD/DO degree, board certifications (Obesity Medicine, Internal Medicine, Endocrinology), and medical license details.
  - **Physician & Nurse Practitioner Network:** Bios and credentials of licensed providers.
  - **Clinical Philosophy:** Commitment to safe titration, muscle mass preservation, and metabolic health.

---

### 9. Patient Self-Injection Video Guide (`/guides/how-to-inject`)
* **Objective:** Eliminate needle fear—the #1 operational cause of customer churn and support tickets.
* **Key Elements Needed:**
  - **Embedded Step-by-Step Video:** Short 90-second instructional video demonstrating subcutaneous self-injection.
  - **Injection Sites Diagram:** Visual map of recommended injection areas (Abdomen 2 inches away from belly button, Front of thigh, Back of upper arm).
  - **Step-by-Step Checklist:** Wash hands → Alcohol wipe vial & skin → Pinch skin → Inject at 90° angle → Dispose in sharps container.

---

### 10. Managing Side Effects & Nausea Guide (`/guides/side-effects`)
* **Objective:** Proactively prevent first-month drop-off by preparing patients for common gastrointestinal symptoms.
* **Key Elements Needed:**
  - **Common Symptoms & Timeline:** Nausea, mild constipation, indigestion, fatigue (explaining they typically peak on days 1–2 after injection and subside).
  - **Actionable Tips:** Hydration (80–100 oz water/day), prioritizing electrolytes, avoiding greasy/high-fat meals.
  - **Zofran (Ondansetron) Protocol:** When and how to take prescribed anti-nausea tablets.
  - **Red Flag Symptoms:** When to immediately message your provider or seek urgent care (severe abdominal pain, allergic reaction).

---

### 11. Patient Portal Login (`/portal/login`)
* **Objective:** Dedicated dashboard entry point for active patients.
* **Key Elements Needed:**
  - Clean login screen (email + password or 2FA SMS code).
  - Fast access to: Track Current Shipment, Request Dose Adjustment / Refill, Message Medical Team, Update Payment Method.

---

### 12. Privacy Policy & HIPAA Compliance Notice (`/privacy-policy`)
* **Objective:** Legal requirement for handling Protected Health Information (PHI).
* **Key Elements Needed:**
  - Explanation of data encryption (SSL/TLS 256-bit).
  - HIPAA disclosure confirming medical records are never sold or shared with third parties.

---

### 13. Telehealth Informed Consent & Terms of Service (`/terms-of-service`)
* **Objective:** Legal liability protection for remote prescribing and compounded medications.
* **Key Elements Needed:**
  - Telehealth medical consent agreement.
  - Compounded drug disclosure (explaining that compounded medications are custom-prepared and not finished FDA-approved commercial drugs).
  - Subscription cancellation, refill, and refund policy terms.

---

### 14. Order Checkout & Identity Verification (`/checkout`)
* **Objective:** Convert qualified leads into paying subscribers with minimal form friction.
* **Key Elements Needed:**
  - Order summary breakdown showing $0 shipping and transparent monthly rate.
  - Credit/Debit card entry (Stripe / Authorized.net).
  - Photo ID upload field (Driver's License / Passport) for doctor verification.
  - Full body or scale photo upload (if required by your clinical protocol).
