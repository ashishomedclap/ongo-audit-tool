## 2. Deep Dive: How Top Competitors Execute Each Architectural Pillar

### Pillar 1: Trust & Safety Architecture

In the compounded GLP-1 telemedicine space, **Trust is the primary conversion bottleneck**. Prospective patients are anxious about counterfeit medications, non-sterile peptides, scam subscriptions, and side effects.

#### How Leaders Structure Trust Pages:
1. **Compounding Pharmacy Disclosure:**
   - **Mochi Health** hosts dedicated quality and testing pages: `joinmochi.com/blog/aequita-quality-reports` disclosing sterility, endotoxin, and HPLC purity testing.
   - **Lavender Sky Health** maintains transparent per-pharmacy pages disclosing partner facilities (*Hallandale, South Lake, Strive, Empower*) and state-by-state shipping legitimacy.
2. **Dedicated Testimonial & Review Silos:**
   - **OrderlyMeds** built 26+ individual patient testimonial URLs (e.g. `orderlymeds.com/testimonials/arlene-cutter/`, `orderlymeds.com/testimonials/brandy-campbell/`, `orderlymeds.com/testimonials/compounding-medications/`). Each page acts as an organic search landing page for prospective customers searching *"OrderlyMeds real reviews"*.
   - **Mochi Health** has a dedicated URL addressing BBB feedback: `joinmochi.com/blog/mochi-health-bbb-reviews-how-we-listen-and-respond`.
3. **FDA & Shortage Compliance Disclosure:**
   - Standard URL pattern: `/fda-compounding-notice/` or `/legal/compounded-medications/`. Explains section 503A/503B of the FD&C Act, clarifying that while compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished drugs, they are legally prepared by state-licensed pharmacies during commercial drug shortages or for custom patient needs.
4. **LegitScript Certification:**
   - All major players (*Ro, LifeMD, Henry Meds, OrderlyMeds*) link their footer seal to their live LegitScript verification registry URL.

### Pillar 2: Doctors & Clinical Team Architecture

To satisfy Google’s **Medical YMYL (Your Money or Your Life)** quality guidelines and build patient confidence, competitors build extensive clinical directories.

#### URL Archetypes Discovered:
* **The Massive Physician Directory Model (Ro):**
  - **Ro** maintains **364+ individual provider landing pages** under:  
    `https://ro.co/network-physician/[physician-name]/`
  - Each physician profile lists:
    - Full Medical Degree (MD, DO)
    - Medical School and Residency credentials
    - State medical licenses held (proving 50-state telehealth compliance)
    - Clinical specialties
* **The Certified Nurse Practitioner & Dietitian Directory (IVÍM Health):**
  - **IVÍM Health** has **72+ dedicated provider pages** under:  
    `https://www.ivimhealth.com/providers/[provider-slug]/`  
    *Examples from sitemap:*
    - `.../providers/aleisha-orr-msn-fnp-c-carn-ap/`
    - `.../providers/allison-buck-msn-aprn-fnp-c/`
    - `.../providers/alyssa-gmitrovic-fnp-c/`
  - Explicitly putting clinical suffixes (`MSN, FNP-C, APRN`) directly into the URL slug signals instant authority to both search algorithms and patients.
* **The Expert Advisory Board Model (OrderlyMeds):**
  - Uses `/expert-advisors/` with individual profiles (e.g., `/expert-advisors/april-foster/`, `/expert-advisors/katie-rickel/`).

### Pillar 3: Authors & E-E-A-T Infrastructure

Google heavily penalizes health websites whose articles are written by anonymous copywriters or AI without verifiable medical review.

#### Competitor Implementations:
1. **Dedicated Author Silos:**
   - **Mochi Health:** Houses all author profiles under `joinmochi.com/blog-authors/` (e.g., `/blog-authors/dr-rebecca-emch-pharmd`, `/blog-authors/eva-shelton`).
   - **TryEden:** Houses authors under `/in/author/[author-name]`.
2. **Two-Tier Bylines:**
   Every high-ranking blog article features a dual byline:
   - **"Written by [Health Writer / Journalist]"**
   - **"Medically Reviewed by [Dr. Name, MD / PharmD / Board-Certified Obesity Specialist]"** (with a clickable link to their medical bio and date of last review).
3. **Medical Advisory Board Seal:**
   A dedicated page explaining the editorial vetting protocol (`/editorial-policy/` or `/medical-review-process/`).

### Pillar 4: Treatment & Product URL Architecture

The way competitors name and structure their product URLs directly balances **SEO volume** with **legal/trademark safety**.

#### Naming Strategies:

| Strategy | Competitor Example | URL Pattern | Rationale |

| **Explicit Descriptive Compound** | JoinFridays | `/products/compounded-glp-1-contains-semaglutide`<br>`/products/compounded-glp-1-gip-contains-tirzepatide` | Avoids trademark lawsuits from Novo Nordisk / Eli Lilly while capturing search intent. |

| **Direct Molecule Silos** | TryEden & Mochi | `/treatment/semaglutide/`<br>`/treatment/tirzepatide/` | Captures high-intent generic searches with clean single-level architecture. |

### Pillar 5: Pricing & Transparency Architecture

Conversion rates drop when pricing is hidden behind a mandatory sign-up wall. Leading competitors use transparent, dedicated pricing URLs:

1. **The Transparent Flat-Rate Landing Page:**
   - `/pricing/` or `/per-dose-pricing/` (e.g. Mochi Health's `joinmochi.com/blog/per-dose-pricing-mochi-health`).
   - Clear side-by-side comparison tables:
     - Semaglutide vs. Tirzepatide.
     - Starter dose price vs. maintenance dose price (explicitly highlighting "NO PRICE INCREASES AS DOSE INCREASES").
2. **HSA / FSA Eligibility Page:**
   - Dedicated landing page: `/hsa-fsa-accepted/`.
   - Explains how patients can use their employer Health Savings Account or Flexible Spending Account cards at checkout, or receive an itemized Superbill / Letter of Medical Necessity (LMN).
3. **Insurance Navigation vs. Compounded Direct Pay:**
   - Competitors like LifeMD, Sequence, and Zealthy maintain dual URLs:
     - `/insurance-check/` (for brand-name Wegovy/Zepbound prior authorization).
     - `/compounded-plans/` (when insurance denies coverage, immediately routing to cash-pay compounded delivery).

### Pillar 6: Conversion Funnels & Asynchronous Intake

The onboarding funnel is the financial engine of the business. Competitors structure their funnel pages to minimize bounce rates:

1. **The 90-Second Qualification Quiz:**
   - Canonical URLs: `/quiz`, `/get-started`, `/assessment`, `/eligibility`.
   - Step 1: Zip code & state verification (validating state licensing).
   - Step 2: Biological sex, height, weight, target weight (calculates BMI instantly).
   - Step 3: Contraindication screening (Thyroid cancer / MEN 2, pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, pregnancy).
   - Step 4: Compound preference (Semaglutide vs Tirzepatide; Injections vs Oral).
   - Step 5: Email/phone capture *before* displaying pricing options (enabling automated SMS/email cart-abandonment recovery).
2. **Asynchronous Medical Charting:**
   - `/id-verification/`: Secure upload of government-issued photo ID and scale/full-body photo.
   - Eliminates the need for a synchronous 30-minute Zoom call, allowing licensed clinicians to review and approve prescriptions in batches within 12–24 hours.

### Pillar 7: Patient Education & Retention Guides

The highest churn in GLP-1 telemedicine occurs between **Days 7 and 30** due to:
- Injection anxiety (fear of self-administering shots).
- Nausea / gastrointestinal distress.
- Misunderstanding vial concentrations / syringe markings (insulin units vs milligrams).

#### Competitor Solutions in Sitemaps:
* **Lavender Sky Health** has built an entire educational guide hub:
  - `https://lavenderskyhealth.com/self-injection-guide/`
  - `https://lavenderskyhealth.com/subq-injection-guide/`
  - `https://lavenderskyhealth.com/read-verify-vials-guide/` (how to read compounding pharmacy vial labels)
  - `https://lavenderskyhealth.com/side-effects-faq-guide/`
  - `https://lavenderskyhealth.com/maintenance/` (how to taper off or maintain weight long-term)
* **Ro** created 800+ dedicated weight-loss guide pages:
  - `https://ro.co/weight-loss/semaglutide-storage/` (refrigeration & shelf life)
  - `https://ro.co/weight-loss/can-i-start-zepbound-at-5mg/` (titration management)
