# Ongo Weight Loss: Interactive Tools, Content Silos & Internal Linking Blueprint

## Executive Summary & Strategic Vision
**Ongo Weight Loss** (`ongoweightloss.com`) is evolving from a standard telehealth consultation website into a **dominant, high-converting organic patient-acquisition engine** for compounded GLP-1 medications (compounded Semaglutide and Tirzepatide).

In direct-to-consumer healthcare, relying strictly on paid media (Meta/Google Ads) creates unsustainable customer acquisition costs ($300–$500+ CAC). Market leaders win by building an **organic conversion moat**:
1. **Interactive Self-Service Tools** that capture high-intent searchers at zero media cost.
2. **Topical Hub-and-Spoke Silos** that dominate Google for transactional, commercial, and clinical queries.
3. **Strict Internal Linking Equity Loops** that funnel traffic from top-of-funnel informational queries directly into the clinical intake flow (`/weightloss-onboard/`).

## 3. Internal Linking Architecture (The Equity Loop)

Internal links must never be placed randomly. They follow a strict 4-tier linking matrix:

### Rule 1: Vertical Funneling (Spokes Pass Equity to Hubs & Money Pages)
* Every informational article in Silos 4 & 7 must contain:
  1. **One contextual link in the first 150 words** to the corresponding Core Treatment page (`/services/glp-1/compounded-semaglutide/` or `/services/glp-1/compounded-tirzepatide/`).
  2. **One contextual link to an interactive tool** (e.g., dosing guides link to `/tools/glp1-dosage-converter/`).
  3. **A prominent floating/sticky conversion banner** pointing directly to `/weightloss-onboard/`.

### Rule 2: Tools Are Conversion Bridges
* Every Interactive Tool in Silo 2 acts as an intermediate conversion mechanism:
  - Input Step -> Calculation -> Instant Result Screen.
  - The result screen **must feature a prominent 1-click CTA button**:
    *"Lock in your plan & claim doctor consult"* -> passes user's selected medication directly to `/weightloss-onboard/?med=semaglutide&dose=starter`.

### Rule 3: E-E-A-T Medical Authority Stamp on Every Page
* Every guide, dosing chart, and tool must feature:
  - **"Medically Reviewed by Dr. [Name], MD"** right below the H1 heading.
  - The doctor's name must link directly to their dedicated bio in Silo 5 (e.g., `/medical-team/dr-david-okonkwo/`).
  - The doctor's bio page in turn links back to the core treatments and states they are licensed in.

### Rule 4: Lateral Silo Cross-Linking (Decision Paths)
* Dosing guides must cross-link to the Cost Calculator and Comparison pages:
  - *Example:* On `/blog/semaglutide/semaglutide-dosing-guide/`, include: *"Unsure if semaglutide or tirzepatide fits your weight goals? Use our [Semaglutide vs Tirzepatide Decision Matcher](/tools/semaglutide-vs-tirzepatide-quiz/) to compare 15% vs 22% average weight loss."*
