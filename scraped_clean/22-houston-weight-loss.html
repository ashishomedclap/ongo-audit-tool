How the Ongo Weight Loss Program Works

Getting started should feel simple.

  1. 5 to 15 minutes
    Step 1

    Complete Your Assessment

    Answer a few questions about your health history, goals, medications, and overall health. It takes about 5 to 15 minutes.

  2. Video or phone
    Step 2

    Talk to a Board-Certified Provider

    Meet with a licensed provider by video or phone. They’ll review your health information and lab results to determine whether treatment is medically appropriate. If prescribed, fill your prescription at a pharmacy or choose Ongo Weight Loss for medication delivery and ongoing care.

  3. Ongoing care
    Step 3

    Start Treatment with Ongoing Care

    Choose the treatment plan that works for you. If prescribed through Ongo, your medication is shipped in discreet packaging from a licensed U.S. pharmacy with tracking. Your care team provides regular check-ins, answers your questions, and makes treatment adjustments when medically appropriate.
